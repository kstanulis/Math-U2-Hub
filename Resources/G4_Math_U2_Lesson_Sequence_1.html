Teacher Notes — Carry Through the Whole Unit This sequence is built primarily from Illustrative Mathematics Grade 4 Unit 6 (Multiplying and Dividing Multi-digit Numbers) — the "IM L#" tag on each card names the specific IM lesson(s) that day draws on. Bridges is used only to fill three genuine gaps IM Unit 6 doesn't cover on its own: the explicit ten-times place-value teaching IM assumes from Grade 3 (Day 2), an extra practice day before the first Checkpoint (Day 7), and doubling/halving efficiency reasoning (Day 12) — each marked "Bridges Supplement" in its tags. Problem-Solving Conferring runs every day, named in the check-in row. All three Bridges Checkpoints stay exactly as written and unmoved in their role as the assessment spine. This card sequence names which IM lesson to teach each day — full IM-formatted lesson plans (warm-up, activities, synthesis, cool-down, MLR supports) for each day are a natural follow-on task.
