# Learning Assets — Grade 4 Reference

Used in the right-hand panel of every split-screen learning intention.
Badge format: `[Category] · [Disposition]`
Question format: "As [category plural], how can being **[disposition]** help us…"

---

## Thinkers
Students who think carefully, persistently, and with an open mind.

| Disposition | When to use |
|-------------|-------------|
| Resilient | Bouncing back from difficulty; not giving up when the work is hard |
| Persistent | Sticking with hard work; rereading; revising; trying again |
| Open-Minded | Reconsidering a first idea; looking at a problem from a different angle |

---

## Researchers
Students who investigate, observe closely, and seek out information.

| Disposition | When to use |
|-------------|-------------|
| Curious | Noticing something interesting; asking a genuine question |
| Observant | Noticing details in a text, image, or situation that others might miss |
| Resourceful | Using tools, texts, or classmates to find what they need |

---

## Communicators
Students who express themselves clearly and listen and respond respectfully.

| Disposition | When to use |
|-------------|-------------|
| Confident | Speaking or reading aloud; sharing writing or ideas publicly |
| Responsive | Adjusting what they say based on their audience; building on what someone else said |
| Respectful | Listening fully before responding; disagreeing without putting someone down |

---

## Collaborators
Students who work well with and for others.

| Disposition | When to use |
|-------------|-------------|
| Reliable | Following through on their role in a group or partnership |
| Empathetic | Genuinely trying to understand another person's perspective |
| Compassionate | Noticing when someone needs help or encouragement, and offering it |

---

## Self Managers
Students who take ownership of their learning, behaviour, and wellbeing, and adjust when things change.

| Disposition | When to use |
|-------------|-------------|
| Responsible | Taking care of materials, notebooks, routines, and commitments |
| Flexible | Trying a new strategy; switching approaches when stuck; adjusting when plans change |
| Reflective | Stepping back to notice their own thinking, habits, or growth |

---

## Selection rules (summary)

- No two consecutive lesson cards may share the same category + disposition pair
- Part 1 (community / habits): weight toward **Self Managers** and **Researchers**
- Part 2 (skill / strategy): weight toward **Thinkers** and **Communicators**
- Part 3 (application / discussion): weight toward **Collaborators**, **Researchers**, **Self Managers**
- Final day of every unit: **Self Managers · Reflective** — always
